## Signature, toolkit and field notes

- Signature artifact: the original floating Crown arena and rift monument, backed by functional collision and combat.
- Atmosphere: full 3D sky/islands, polygon scenery and active gameplay, not a flat page of boxes.
- Layered depth: world, player/equipment, HUD and deliberate pause/modal layers.
- Product artifact: the game itself, including real state and working controls.
- Character type: heavy, compact game display treatment, with neutral readable instructions.
- Bespoke silhouette: custom rift mark, portal geometry, district structures, swords and relics.
- Treated navigation: compact battle-menu controls, not a generic marketing header.
- Specifics: actual weapon names, costs, timers, player identities and match state. No fake metrics.
- Cohesion: bright world materials, rarity-coded gear and blue/yellow game menus honor the revised brief.
- Signature serif / two-tone headline: not suitable for the latest battle-game direction; omitted.
- Full-bleed atmosphere: supplied by the functioning Three.js world.
- Animated character field / gradient icons: not relevant; omitted.
- Arrows: not scattered as decoration; controls use concise action labels.
- Glass CTA: not relevant; omitted.
- Component foundations: native accessible buttons, forms, selects, ranges, checkboxes and modal dialogs. No decorative fake controls.
- Prebuilt styling: no untouched prebuilt marketing blocks or theme switch.
- Creative medium: one consistent polygonal 3D medium plus original functional vectors.
- Type selection: no repeated Google-font rotation; the revised user's desired game energy guides the treatment.
- Reference language: Fortnite-like colors and inventory interaction, with original arena, player, weapons, text and code.
- Motion: visible by default; reduced-motion preference removes nonessential scene drift and weapon bob. Essential game action feedback remains visible.

## Defects found and corrected during the re-check

- Replaced overlapping ground/road/plaza geometry with one partitioned surface; removed coplanar roof/planter/stair/pad overlays. Geometry audit: zero cross-mesh overlapping coplanar upward faces, exact 84×84 terrain coverage.
- Teleport movement now carries acknowledgement numbers; stale movement cannot undo AOTE.
- Removed the stale jump latch, increased double-jump feedback, and verified both jumps.
- Added bounded physics substeps so high-speed grapples and boosted movement cannot tunnel through thin walls.
- Movement packets explicitly copy only allowed properties; they cannot overwrite health, score, identity or room modifiers.
- Removed the lobby avatar name that overlapped navigation; resized the avatar/platform at phone widths.
- Added a persistent low-health border and stronger text contrast over the ground.
- Fixed gun aim/camera direction agreement and initial guest facing direction.
- Prevented captured-game Ctrl key combinations from activating browser shortcuts.
- Revised audio to soft arcade feedback and added swap/reload motion.
- Kept host-only modifier controls synchronized across the room.
